Applying this to your situation

Your inheritance ≈ house (base case $5,435,869 in 2045) plus cash. Federal tax is owed only on the part of the estate above the exclusion at death. With the projected 2045 exclusion of ≈$23,980,000 per person (≈$47,960,000 per couple via portability), a 'considerable' cash amount still fits below the line unless the combined estate exceeds it. If it might: (1) confirm both spouses file/consider Form 706 for portability even if not required; (2) monitor Congress — the exclusion is permanent now but future legislation could lower it; (3) lifetime gifts use the same exclusion ($19,000/yr per recipient free of reporting).
