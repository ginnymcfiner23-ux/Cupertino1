Why this conclusion

Three levers dominate: (1) Prop 19 — the inherited low tax basis only survives if a child moves in (this is worth ≈$55k/yr in 2045 dollars). (2) Step-up basis — selling right after inheritance costs ≈$0 in capital-gains tax, so the full ≈$5.4M (base case) is redeployable. (3) Return spread — a diversified portfolio (6% assumption; 10-yr Treasury ≈4.84% as a risk-free anchor) vs. a single house (3% assumption) plus $58k/yr carrying costs makes selling win in most worlds. The lifestyle value of living in the house (worth ≈$9,600/mo rent in 2045) is real but is consumption, not profit.
