Two things only the owner can fix

  • F16 — the original spreadsheet. The year-by-year tax and assessment tables for both properties were not available when this site was built, so most of those cells are blank. Supplying the two dollar columns for 2026–2014 (Cupertino) and 2025–2016 (San Francisco) restores them verbatim.
  • F12 — the original purchase basis. Without the purchase price and purchase date for each property, the decision to sell during the owner’s lifetime (which avoids reassessment entirely) cannot be quantified against the decision to keep and reassess.

How these were found

Cross-checking owner data

Every owner-supplied tax and assessment figure was divided by the matching official rate to get an implied rate, and compared with the published rate for the relevant county and fiscal year. Three of the four discrepancies on this page were found that way (F1, F4, F5).

Re-deriving published rates

The Cupertino and San Francisco bills were rebuilt from the published rate schedules — the San Jose Water Company CPUC tariff, the SFPUC FY2026-27 water and sewer schedules, the Cupertino Sanitary District rate — and the totals compared with the owner’s observed bills. That is how F6, F7, F7b and F16b surfaced.

Reading the statute

The trust question was traced from Revenue and Taxation Code §62(d) through BOE Legal Letter Advice 80/25 and BOE Legal Opinion 220.0791, and cross-checked against the Santa Clara County Assessor’s own statement that inheritance from a living trust is reassessed as of the date of death (F10).

How to read the badges

  • owner supplied by the owner — not verified
  • derived computed from other stated figures
  • assumption a planning assumption with no official source
  • unverified could not be confirmed — left blank
  • pending not yet published or not yet in force

Privacy

No name, street address, parcel number, account number or any other personal identifier appears anywhere on this site. The two properties are referred to only as PROPERTY_CUPERTINO and PROPERTY_SF; the owner as OWNER.

This dashboard is decision-support analysis, not legal, tax or investment advice. Every figure is either traced to the official source linked beside it or flagged as unverified.
