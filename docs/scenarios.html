Option comparison

How to use this table

Compare the two “base preserved” lines against the two “reassessed” lines for each property. If the base is preserved, holding wins comfortably on both properties — the Cupertino house because it is a cheap shelter for a large appreciating asset, the San Francisco building because the tax stays negligible. If the base is lost, holding the San Francisco building collapses and selling it after the inheritance wins; holding the Cupertino house still beats selling it if the heirs can live in it and claim Prop 19, because the §1014 step-up makes the taxable gain on a sale so small.

Every assumption

How to read the model

What the NPV actually compares

For a hold option, the NPV is the discounted value of the annual net cash flow over the horizon plus the discounted terminal market value, measured at the inheritance year. For PROPERTY_CUPERTINO the annual cash flow is the market rent (as a benefit, whether received or simply avoided) less operating costs and property tax. For PROPERTY_SF it is the net operating income from the two rented units; the owner-occupied third unit is not valued as rent, which makes the hold option conservative.

For a sell option, the NPV is the net sale proceeds (after transfer taxes, sale costs and capital-gain tax on the gain over the §1014 stepped-up basis) grown at the reinvestment return and discounted back to the inheritance year.

What it deliberately does not do

  • It does not value the owner-occupied Cupertino house as housing consumed.
  • It does not value the owner’s own labour in maintaining the San Francisco building.
  • It does not model a lifetime sale, because the original purchase basis was not supplied (flag F12).
  • It does not model the FAIR Plan, a decline-in-value year, or a Prop 8 temporary reduction.
  • It uses the owner’s $1,800,000 and $3,100,000 market estimates; those are estimates, not appraisals.

How to read the badges

  • official confirmed against a government or official rate source
  • owner supplied by the owner — not verified
  • derived computed from other stated figures
  • assumption a planning assumption with no official source
  • unverified could not be confirmed — left blank
  • pending not yet published or not yet in force

Privacy

No name, street address, parcel number, account number or any other personal identifier appears anywhere on this site. The two properties are referred to only as PROPERTY_CUPERTINO and PROPERTY_SF; the owner as OWNER.

This dashboard is decision-support analysis, not legal, tax or investment advice. Every figure is either traced to the official source linked beside it or flagged as unverified. The figures marked “owner” are the owner’s own estimates and have not been independently confirmed. Confirm the trust, Prop 19 and estate-tax conclusions with a California estate-planning attorney before acting.  ·  Data as of 6 November 2026.
