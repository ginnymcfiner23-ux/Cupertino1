Property facts and running costs

The private source dataset includes a California home and an income-producing multi-unit property. Their locations, rent, valuations, historical tax figures, utilities and unit-level details are intentionally masked in this public version. The data owner should privately compare records with official county bills before relying on them.

Projection policy: No numerical 2040–2050 property-value or cost forecast is shown. A forward projection requires assumptions (appreciation, CPI, tax rates, insurance coverage and utility inflation); no official source can certify those future values, and property-specific bills or quotes are private. Legally sourced formulas and required inputs are provided below.

Forecast method

What a defensible projection needs

Market value and taxable assessed value differ

For scenario analysis, market value may be represented as Vy = Vbase × (1 + g)years. The starting value and growth assumption are private / unverified and intentionally omitted. Assessed value follows base-year and Prop 13 rules, not the market-growth formula; after transfer the County Assessor determines the new base-year value and any Prop 19 exclusion.

Tax bill = taxable assessed value × applicable local rate + applicable direct assessments / charges

SF official assessment formula

Annual owner cost

Include property tax, homeowner or dwelling-fire insurance, water, electricity/net-metering, routine maintenance, capital repairs, HOA/garbage/sewer if applicable, and rent loss / vacancy. For mixed personal/rental use, allocate eligible expenses using tax guidance and actual unit use.

Net rent = collected rent − vacancy − tax − insurance − utilities − repairs − management − financing − income tax

IRS Pub. 527 · CDI insurance data
Insurance research result: California DOI publishes ZIP-level experience data by policy form, including owner-occupied and tenant-occupied dwelling-fire policies. That dataset is aggregate historical experience—not a quote for a particular dwelling, building limits, construction, deductible, or number of units. No premium estimate is asserted. Obtain written quotes with matching replacement-cost limits and confirm rental / owner-occupied use. CDI: coverage and pricing factors
