Prop 19 consequence for the Cupertino house

Because the market value (≈ $3.1M) exceeds the assessed value (≈ $550,537) by far more than the Prop 19 cap (assessed value + $1M, biennially adjusted), the tax results if the house is inherited around 2040–2050:

  • Rented or kept as a second home: full reassessment to market value at transfer → property tax jumps to ≈ 1.15% × market value (≈ $35,650/yr at 2026 values; far more by 2040–2050 as values grow).
  • Heir moves in as primary residence: exclusion may apply only if the heir occupies within 1 year and files BOE-266 (1 yr) + BOE-19-P (3 yrs); even then the new assessed value ≈ market value − $1M cap → ≈ $2.1M assessed at 2026 values → ≈ $24,150/yr tax, rising with value.

Sources: CA BOE Proposition 19; BOE Letter to Assessors 2026/026 (value cap = factored base year value + $1M, adjusted every other February 16 starting 2023); IRS Pub 559 (reassessment of inherited property as of date of death per county Assessor practice; see also Santa Clara County Assessor booklet).
