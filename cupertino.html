Proposition 19 impact if Property A is inherited (public rules)

California Proposition 19 (effective Feb 16, 2021) governs parent-to-child transfers:

  • Kept as rental / second home (not transferee's primary residence): fully reassessed to market value at transfer; no parent-child exclusion. [CA BOE Prop 19]
  • Transferee moves in as primary residence: exclusion only if the transferee occupies within 1 year, files the homeowner's exemption (BOE-266) within 1 year, and files the reassessment-exclusion claim (BOE-19-P) within 3 years (or before transfer to a third party). Even then, the protected amount is capped at the factored base year value + $1M, adjusted biennially by the BOE. Above the cap, the excess is reassessed. [BOE LTA 2026/026]
  • Continued eligibility: the home must remain the transferee's primary residence; moving out and converting to rental ends the exclusion. [BOE LTA 2026/026 — Continual Use]

Because the excluded amount is capped near the old assessed value plus $1M, a property whose market value far exceeds its assessed value will generally see a material property-tax increase under Prop 19 unless the exclusion fully applies. Compute privately with the enrolled assessed value and the current BOE cap.
