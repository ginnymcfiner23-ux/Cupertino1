# Cupertino1

House inheritance decision dashboard — Cupertino, CA (ZIP 95014) and San Francisco, CA (ZIP 94122).

## Purpose

Collect, organize, and analyze the requirements for deciding what to do with a Cupertino house planned to pass by **trust inheritance around 2040–2050**: live in it, rent it, or sell it and reinvest the proceeds. The existing San Francisco home (three units, two rented) is kept in the decision frame.

## Key findings (Nov 2026)

- **California has no inheritance tax and no state estate tax; inheritance is not taxable income.** Source: CA Franchise Tax Board (ftb.ca.gov); CA Revenue & Taxation Code §13301.
- **Federal estate tax exemption (2026): $15,000,000 per person; $30,000,000 per married couple with portability; top rate 40%.** Source: IRS.gov, Form 706 instructions. Set by Pub. L. 119-21 §70106 (One Big Beautiful Bill Act, signed July 4, 2025), amending IRC §2010(c); indexed for inflation after 2026.
- **Step-up in basis:** inherited property basis resets to fair market value at date of death → selling shortly after inheritance generally produces little or no capital gains tax. Source: IRS Publication 551 (IRC §1014).
- **Proposition 19 (effective Feb 16, 2021):** keeping an inherited California home as a rental/second home triggers full reassessment to market value; an heir who moves in as primary residence (file BOE-266 within 1 year, BOE-19-P within 3 years) keeps assessed value + $1M cap (biennially adjusted). Source: CA Board of Equalization, boe.ca.gov/prop19.
- **Bottom line (illustrative, not advice):** sell the Cupertino house shortly after inheritance and reinvest; keep the San Francisco home (low assessed value + ≈ $7,150/month rental income while living in the third unit). Keeping the Cupertino house as a rental is projected to carry high property tax after reassessment — likely above achievable rent.

## Verification standard

Every table row on the site links to an official source (irs.gov, ftb.ca.gov, boe.ca.gov, sccassessor.org, sfassessor.org, sfpuc.gov, sjwater.com/CPUC, pge.com/CPUC, insurance.ca.gov, congress.gov, courts.ca.gov, leginfo.legislature.ca.gov, fhfa.gov), or the cell is left blank and flagged as unverified on the Flagged for Review page. No values are inferred or filled in. Projections are explicitly labeled assumptions and are never presented as verified facts.
