Why the public site shows formulas instead of your numbers

Per the privacy instruction, property-specific values (market value, assessed value, tax history, rent, utilities, insurance) and any projection computed from them are withheld from this website. The formulas below show exactly how to recompute every figure privately once the official, property-specific values are known.

Projection formulas

QuantityFormulaNotes / source of inputs
Projected market value in year t Valuet = Value0 × (1 + g)t Value0 = market value at transfer (withheld). g = annual appreciation assumption — no official source for future appreciation; model privately with low/mid/high scenarios.
Assessed value while not reassessed AVt = AV0 × (1.02)t Prop 13 cap: lower of inflation or 2% per year absent ownership change/new construction [SCC Assessor booklet] · CA Const. art. XIII A
Property tax after reassessment Tax = Assessed value × combined effective rate Santa Clara County combined residential effective rates typically ≈ 1.08–1.30%; SF combined ≈ 1.1763% (FY 2024–25). Set by Tax Rate Area; verify per parcel [SCC] · [SF]
Prop 19 — transferee moves in (primary residence) New AV ≈ market value − $1M cap (cap adjusted biennially by BOE), when market value exceeds base value + cap CA BOE Prop 19 · [BOE LTA 2026/026] · occupancy within 1 year; BOE-266 within 1 year; BOE-19-P within 3 years
Prop 19 — kept as rental / not primary residence Full reassessment to market value at transfer CA BOE Prop 19
Escalating annual cost Costt = Cost0 × (1 + i)t i = expense-inflation assumption (e.g., 3%/yr — planning assumption, not an official forecast)
Net sale proceeds Proceeds = Sale price × (1 − selling-cost %) Selling cost % is a planning assumption (e.g., 6%); step-up in basis generally resets capital-gains basis to date-of-death FMV [IRS Pub 551] · [IRS Pub 559]
Reinvestment growth FV = Proceeds × (1 + r)n r = portfolio-return assumption — illustrative only, not a guarantee or official forecast

Projection outputs — withheld

Year-by-year property value, property tax, insurance, water, electricity, and maintenance projections for Properties A and B are computed from withheld property-specific inputs and are therefore not published on this site.

To build your own projection: obtain the enrolled assessed values and tax bills from the county Assessors, a date-of-death-style market appraisal, insurance quotes, utility statements, and lease ledgers; then apply the formulas above. Re-run the model at the actual transfer date — rules and thresholds (including the federal estate exclusion) can change before 2040–2050.
