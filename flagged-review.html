Data integrity

Irregularities / cautions noticed

Assessed value and market value are different measures.

Private source figures included assessment values below market estimates. California Prop 13 may produce a gap between assessed and market values, so that difference alone does not prove an error. Verify each property and year from the county record before using any figure.

SF explanation of Prop 13 assessments · SCC Assessor
Tax entries need parcel-specific reconciliation.

Historical annual tax changes and assessed-value changes are supplied separately and cannot be validated from a city or ZIP code. A tax bill may include bonds, exemptions, direct assessments, supplemental bills or other components. Do not estimate a transfer-year bill by applying a generic rate.

SF Treasurer property-tax information · FY 2025–26 SF rate resolution
