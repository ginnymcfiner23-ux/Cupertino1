  • Federal law may change: the $15M estate tax exemption (OBBBA, Pub. L. 119-21) is permanent under current law but indexed and subject to future legislation; proposals exist (e.g., H.R. 2576/2676 "For the 99.5 Percent Act," STEP Act, Death Tax Repeal Act) — none enacted. Track at congress.gov.

How to resolve these flags

  1. Record the parcel APN(s) internally (not on this site).
  2. Pull the enrolled assessed value and prior tax bills from the Santa Clara County Assessor/Treasurer-Tax Collector and the SF Assessor-Recorder/Treasurer-Tax Collector.
  3. Obtain actual insurance declarations pages and utility bills (SJWC, PG&E, SFPUC).
  4. Replace each "unverified" value with the verified figure and re-run the projections page.
  5. Review the estate (house + cash + other assets) against the federal estate tax exemption with a CPA/estate attorney before the transfer.
