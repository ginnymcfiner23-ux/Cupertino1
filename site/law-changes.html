Law Changes That Could Affect This Decision

Property and inheritance law is not static. Items below are tracked because they could change the numbers in Projections and Decision. Each item is marked verified, pending, or unverified.

1. Recently enacted — federal estate tax (verification: official)

ChangeWhat happenedEffect on this planStatus
One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA), Public Law 119-21, signed July 4, 2025Set the federal estate/gift basic exclusion at $15,000,000 per person starting in 2026 (amending IRC §2010(c)(3)), permanent rather than temporary, indexed for inflation after 2027. The TCJA's scheduled post-2025 sunset (~$7M level) was canceled.The $15M exclusion is less likely to drop in the near term, but a future Congress could still change it. If total estate < $15M/person ($30M couple with portability), no federal estate tax.Verified · IRS Form 706 instructions; Rev. Proc. 2025-32
TCJA estate-tax doubling (2017)Doubled the exclusion from $5M to $10M (inflation-adjusted), scheduled to expire after Dec 31, 2025Superseded by OBBBA for 2026 onwardVerified · IRS

2. Pending — California Proposition 19 repeal initiative (verification: pending/unverified)

ChangeWhat would happenStatusEffect on this plan
Voter initiative to repeal Prop 19's inheritance restrictions ("Repeal the Death Tax" / "Fix Prop 19")Would restore the pre-2021 parent-child exclusion: heirs could inherit the family home and keep the parent's low Prop 13 assessed value without a residency requirement or $1M cap.Unverified — reported signature gathering for the November 2026 ballot; not confirmed to have qualifiedIf passed, keeping the Cupertino home becomes far more attractive (low taxes preserved without moving in). Until it qualifies and passes, Prop 19 is fully in effect.
