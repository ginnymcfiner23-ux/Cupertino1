Prop 19 and Prop 13 — how they apply here

Inheritance is a change in ownership (BOE). Without an exclusion, the property is reassessed to market value. A child may keep the parent's base-year value if the child makes the home a principal residence within one year, under the $1,044,586 cap (BOE). Rental and other real property are not excluded. Prop 13 caps later growth at 2% a year on the factored base-year value.
