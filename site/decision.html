Recommendation

Under current law and this model, sell the Cupertino home shortly after inheritance and reinvest the proceeds, while keeping the San Francisco home as the primary residence with its rental income.

RECOMMENDED: Sell & reinvest — highest projected net income, lowest cost and complexity.
  • Why sell: Proposition 19 removes the old "keep the parents' low tax base" benefit for anyone who doesn't move in. Holding the Cupertino home means reassessed property taxes (materially higher than the current low-basis bill) plus insurance and maintenance, while selling near date-of-death value triggers little or no capital gains tax (stepped-up basis) and puts the proceeds to work.
  • Why not rent: Rentals receive no Prop 19 exclusion → full reassessment. Break-even rent must cover the reassessed tax plus insurance and maintenance before income taxes; achievable rent was not verified.
  • Why not live in it (unless you want to): Only sensible if an heir actually wants to live in Cupertino and can carry reassessed property taxes plus insurance and maintenance. It also ends the SF home's primary-residence status (though a 3rd rentable unit becomes available).
Key uncertainties: future appreciation, tax law (Prop 19 repeal initiative for Nov 2026 ballot is pending; federal estate-tax rules could change), insurance cost, achievable rent, actual maintenance.
