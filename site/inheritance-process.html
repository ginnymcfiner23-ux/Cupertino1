Trust vs. probate: A properly funded living trust avoids probate court entirely — typically saving 6–12 months and tens of thousands of dollars compared with probate. If the house was never deeded into the trust, it may still pass via a Heggstad petition (court confirms intent) or full probate.
Prop 19 warning: simply holding the house in a trust does not preserve the low Prop 13 assessment. For deaths on or after February 16, 2021, an heir must occupy the home as their principal residence within one year and file BOE-19-P to keep even a partial exclusion; rental properties get no exclusion at all.
